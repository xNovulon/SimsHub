**Also in the Hub**

- **Duplicate cleaner.** Removes identical copies of CC, merged files included. Your game shows exactly the same CC.
- **Unmerge.** Splits a merged file (from Sims 4 Studio, Sims 4 Mod Manager or the Hub) back into the files that went into it.
- **Add new downloads.** Drop new CC in one folder and the Hub sorts and merges it safely. Script mods are left alone.
- **Nothing is deleted.** Removed files go to a safe folder, and "Undo last change" brings them back.

**What it can do**

- **Pose by dragging.** Move a hand or foot and the body follows. Ctrl+click several parts to move them together.
- **Faces.** Pose brows, eyes, cheeks and lips by hand, or use ready expressions, blinking and lip-sync.
- **Timeline.** Keys, easing curves, ghost frames, motion trails and seamless loops.
- **Automatic motion.** Physics, sims holding on to each other, and the game's own furniture at the right size.
- **Copy real movement** from a video, your webcam or a motion-capture file.
- **Magic Animation.** Pick a position and a place, and get a finished animation to fine-tune.
- **Sound.** Game sounds, creator sounds or your own audio, placed on the motion automatically.
- **One click to the game.** Send an animation to The Sims 4, or package several as a mod to share.

## Get started

1. Download the app you want with the buttons at the top, and open it.
2. The first start sets everything up and adds a desktop shortcut. It takes a minute or two.
3. From then on the app updates itself whenever you open it.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", select **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Needs:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and The Sims 4 on PC.
